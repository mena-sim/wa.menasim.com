# menasim eSIM FAQ

## What is an eSIM?

An eSIM is a digital SIM built into your phone. You install a data plan by scanning a QR code - no physical SIM card needed.

## Is my phone compatible?

Most iPhone XS/XR and newer, recent Samsung Galaxy, Google Pixel, and many other modern phones support eSIM. Your phone must also be carrier-unlocked. If unsure, check Settings for an "Add eSIM" option.

## When should I install and activate?

Install any time after purchase (while on Wi-Fi). The plan validity/data usually starts when you first connect to a network in the destination, or on the activation date shown on your order. Enable roaming when you arrive.

## Will I be charged for roaming?

No. menasim eSIM plans are prepaid data plans. "Data Roaming" must be ON for the eSIM line to work, but it does not add extra charges on your menasim plan.

## Can I still receive calls and WhatsApp on my normal number?

Yes. Keep your home SIM active for calls/SMS and use the menasim eSIM for data. WhatsApp works over the menasim data connection.

## How do I check my remaining data?

Ask menasim support with your order number or email and we will check the balance for you.

## Can I reuse or reinstall my eSIM?

A QR code can normally only be installed once. Do not delete the eSIM to reinstall it. If you switched phones or deleted it, contact support.

## How do I top up?

Purchase a new plan on the menasim website. Depending on the provider, you may reuse the same eSIM or install a new one - support will advise.
