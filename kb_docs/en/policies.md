# menasim policies (support summary)

## Refunds and cancellations

- An eSIM that has NOT been installed/activated may be eligible for a refund or replacement.
- Once an eSIM has been installed and data has been used, it is generally non-refundable.
- Compatibility issues (phone not eSIM-capable or carrier-locked) are the customer's responsibility, but contact support and we will try to help or offer a replacement where possible.
- To request a refund or cancellation, support will log a ticket with your order number; the billing team reviews and responds. The agent cannot issue refunds automatically.

## Delivery of the eSIM

- The eSIM QR code and instructions are delivered by email and/or WhatsApp shortly after a successful order.
- If you have not received it, check spam, confirm the email/phone on the order, and support can resend it.

## Data and validity

- Each plan lists its data allowance and validity period on the product page and order.
- Validity typically starts on first network connection at the destination or the stated activation date.

## Privacy

- Support may ask for your order number or email to locate your order and eSIM details.
